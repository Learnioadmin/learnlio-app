Cookie Policy

Learnlio uses essential cookies only.

What cookies are used

We use cookies to:

  • Keep you signed in
  • Protect accounts and prevent unauthorised access
  • Ensure the app works correctly

What we don’t use

  • No advertising cookies
  • No marketing trackers
  • No third-party analytics cookies (unless we add them later and update this page)

Managing cookies

You can block cookies in your browser settings. Please note: blocking essential cookies may stop Learnlio from working correctly.
