Child Data & Safeguarding

Last updated: 12/01/2026
Child-first by design
Important: Learnlio is designed for children, but accounts are managed by a parent/guardian. Learnlio does not provide emergency support. If a child is at risk of harm, contact local emergency services.

Our approach

Learnlio is built around a calm, supportive tutoring style. We focus on:

  • Child-led learning
  • Dyslexia-friendly explanations
  • Safe pacing and age-appropriate difficulty
  • Encouragement that rewards effort, not pressure

What data we store for children

  • First name / nickname
  • Date of birth (for age-appropriate content)
  • Learning progress and basic session records

We strongly recommend parents use a first name or nickname only.

What we do not allow

Please do not enter:

  • Full names (surname)
  • Address, phone number, school name
  • Medical records or sensitive personal details

Safety boundaries in tutoring

  • We keep explanations age-appropriate and supportive.
  • We avoid shame-based language and reduce “right/wrong” pressure.
  • We cap pacing by age and promote breaks.

Parent control

  • Parents create and manage child profiles.
  • Parents can view progress and reports.
  • Parents can request deletion of data at any time.

Reporting a concern

If you believe Learnlio content has created a safeguarding concern or you see something that should be improved, contact us immediately:

hello@learnlio.co.uk

Data deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated child profiles by emailing: hello@learnlio.co.uk
