Getting started

Set up once, then keep sessions short and calm.

What is Learnlio?
Learnlio is calm, dyslexia-friendly Maths and English practice for UK families. It is designed to feel safe and encouraging, with clear parent insight.
Who is it for (ages 6-14, UK)?
Learnlio is designed for children aged 6 to 14 in the UK who feel pressured, anxious, or stuck with learning. Sessions are short and confidence-first.
How do I change the selected child?
Go to the Dashboard and use the child picker at the top to choose a different child. The Tutor and Lessons will follow that selection.

Lessons

Short, structured steps with gentle pacing.

How do Lessons work?
Lessons break learning into small steps with quick checks. Children can go at their own pace and repeat steps without pressure.
What is XP and what does it mean?
XP is a simple way to show effort and progress across Tutor and Lessons. It is not a grade and it resets nothing. It is just a calm progress meter.

Tutor

Gentle guidance and short practice sessions.

What is Tutor and when should we use it?
Tutor is a guided practice session for Maths or English. Use it when your child needs a calm, step-by-step approach or a short confidence boost.

Reports

Parent-friendly insight without overload.

How do Reports work?
Reports combine Tutor and Lessons activity into a calm summary. You will see focus areas, steady wins, and gentle next steps.

Billing and trial

Manage your plan in one place.

Billing: where do I manage or cancel?
Open Billing from your Dashboard. You can manage, upgrade, or cancel there. If you need help, contact us and we will assist.

Safeguarding and privacy

Children are protected by design.

Safeguarding: how do you protect children?
Learnlio is designed for calm learning with protective language and pacing. Parent areas are gated and child-facing content avoids pressure.
Privacy: what data is stored?
We store account details, child profiles, and learning progress needed to run the app and reports. We do not sell data. See Privacy for full details.

Troubleshooting

Simple fixes for common issues.

I cant log in - what should I try?
Check your email and password, then try a password reset. If it still does not work, clear your browser cache or try another browser.
Audio or read aloud not working - what should I try?
Check your device volume and browser permissions. Try refreshing the page or switching to Chrome or Edge for the best audio support.
