Quick guide: what helps most

  • Keep it short. 5–10 minutes beats 45 minutes of stress.
  • Finish on a win. Confidence grows when sessions end calmly.
  • Reduce pressure. Wrong answers should feel safe.
  • Use hints early. Don’t wait for frustration to build.
  • Repeat gently. Mastery often needs calm repetition.
