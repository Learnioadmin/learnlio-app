Is tutoring making my child more anxious?

Many parents turn to tutoring to help their child feel more confident — not to add pressure. But sometimes, tutoring can have the opposite effect.

If your child becomes tense, tearful, avoids sessions, or says they “hate learning”, it may be anxiety rather than ability that’s getting in the way.

Signs tutoring may be increasing anxiety

  • Strong resistance before sessions
  • Headaches or tummy aches around learning time
  • Shutting down or refusing to try
  • Negative self-talk like “I’m stupid”

Why tutoring can sometimes backfire

Tutoring increases anxiety when it moves too fast, focuses on mistakes, or mirrors school pressure at home. Children may hear tutoring as “extra help because I’m failing”.

What actually helps anxious learners

Children learn best when sessions are short, calm, and focused on effort rather than results. Feeling safe always comes before progress.
