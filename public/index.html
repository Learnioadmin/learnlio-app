Quick questions

Is Learnlio suitable for anxious children?
Learnlio is designed to protect confidence: calm language, small steps, and no urgency. If your child is very anxious, keep sessions short and stop while it still feels okay.
What ages is Learnlio for?
Learnlio is built for primary and early secondary learners. Sessions are paced and capped by age so children don’t get overwhelmed.
Is this a replacement for school or tutoring?
No. Learnlio is learning support at home — helpful for practice, confidence, and gentle guidance. It can sit alongside school and tutoring.
Can my child use it on a phone or tablet?
Yes. Learnlio is a web app that works on phone, tablet, and laptop. Progress stays with your account.
