Dyslexia-friendly tutoring in the UK

Learnlio is built for UK families looking for a dyslexia-friendly tutor that supports children in Maths and English. It’s designed to be calm and structured for learners who struggle with working memory, speed, confidence, and reading/spelling.

Whether your child needs help with number bonds, addition and subtraction, times tables, spelling, or sentence building, Learnlio guides them step-by-step and saves progress to their profile.

Quick FAQ

Is Learnlio a dyslexia diagnosis?
No. The screener is for early insight and risk signs. It doesn’t diagnose. If you’re concerned, speak to your school or a qualified professional.
What ages is Learnlio for?
Learnlio is designed for primary and early secondary learners. Sessions are paced and capped by age.
Can I use Learnlio on phone, tablet, and laptop?
Yes. Learnlio is a web app and your child’s progress is stored to your account so you can pick up on another device.
Do children have to use voice?
No. Voice only plays when the child presses the Listen button.
