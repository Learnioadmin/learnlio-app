Start a free trial

Start a 7-day trial from the Billing page. No card is required to begin.

If it helps, you can subscribe at any time during the trial.

Still stuck? Visit Contact or email hello@learnlio.co.uk.

Subscribe for GBP 0.99 per month

After the trial, you can subscribe for GBP 0.99 per month.

You can cancel anytime from the billing portal.

Still stuck? Visit Contact or email hello@learnlio.co.uk.

Manage or cancel in the billing portal

Use the Billing page to open the Stripe portal. From there you can update payment details or cancel.

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period.

Still stuck? Visit Contact or email hello@learnlio.co.uk.

Next payment due date

When your subscription is active, the Billing page shows the next payment due date.

It is based on the current period end from Stripe.

Still stuck? Visit Contact or email hello@learnlio.co.uk.

Payment methods

Payments are handled by Stripe. Card payments are supported and Apple Pay or Google Pay may appear when available.

If a method does not appear, it may not be enabled for your device or browser.

Still stuck? Visit Contact or email hello@learnlio.co.uk.

I paid but still see limited access

First, refresh the Billing page. If the status still does not update, sign out and back in.

Payments can take a short time to sync. If it continues, contact support.

Still stuck? Visit Contact or email hello@learnlio.co.uk.
